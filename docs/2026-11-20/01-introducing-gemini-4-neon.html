Introducing Gemini 4 Neon

Google DeepMind · 1 天前
Nov 18, 2026 | x.com Facebook LinkedIn Mail Copy link Bringing frontier-level intelligence with fast end-to-end task completion across complex software engineering, knowledge work, and science workflows. Koray Kavukcuoglu SVP, Google DeepMind and Chief AI Architect, Google Share x.com Facebook LinkedIn Mail Copy link In this article Today, we’re introducing Gemini 4 Neon — bringing frontier-level intelligence with fast end-to-end task completion across complex software engineering, knowledge work, and science workflows. Neon ranks ahead of comparable models and #3 overall with a score of 61 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at its xhigh thinking level, landing just behind larger, slower models. On the Vals Index , Neon ranks #2 and lands on the Pareto frontier. Beyond just pushing on raw model capability, we’re continuing to make our models better to work with, Neon ranks #2 on steerability (adapting cleanly to mid-task user corrections) on the new Agent Arena Index . Neon also excels at multi-step instruction following and builds on our ongoing progress to curb fabrication, recording our lowest fabrication rate yet in internal Google Antigravity testing. The model is priced at $3 per million input tokens ($0.15 per million cached input tokens, a 95% discount) and $15 per million output tokens, with a 1 million-token context window. Gemini 4 Neon is rolling out today in the Gemini API in Google AI Studio , Gemini for business (via API and model dropdown), for paid Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers in Google Antigravity , and for Pro and Ultra subscribers in the Gemini app . Delivers across enterprise, analytical, and scientific work Enterprise work lives in spreadsheets, PDFs, charts, audio, and video. Neon can synthesize across all of these inputs in a single 1 million-token context window and turns inputs into structured deliverables. Independent evaluations rank Neon as one of the top models and on benchmarks like the Vals Index , it ranks #2 (70.9%), and reaches #1 on both the Legal Research Bench and Tax Agent Bench . Neon also shows strong wins across categories on Surge AI , ranking #1 on most benchmarks across finance, accounting, spreadsheet modeling, and complex constraint following. It also scores 73.5% on Chartography for reading dense professional charts and performs on par with leading models on Agents' Last Exam v1.1 (75.8%). It achieves #1 overall on both Zapier’s AutomationBench (56.3%) and Andon Labs' Vending-Bench 2 ($16,843), a long-horizon simulation of running a business. Finally, it debuts #2 globally with a score of 1336 on Arena's new Agent Arena Index across 4,200+ real-world agent sessions. On scientific and mathematical reasoning, spanning quantitative analysis, mathematics, and technical research problems, Neon is best in class on Terminal-Bench Science (70.5%), RiemannBench (84.0%), and HLE-Diamond (71.3%). Built for production-ready code Engineered for long-running agent workflows, Neon sustains performance through complex, tool-heavy tasks end-to-end — whether running standalone or acting as a fast, high-judgment Orchestrator directing parallel Hydrogen subagents in Google Antigravity. It cuts down on redundant file reads, shares progress as it works, and handles context compaction reliably in long sessions. Across core developer and researcher workflows, Neon achieves frontier performance and is competitive with the leading models in its class, ranking #1 overall on the Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index (58.6), with a score of 65.2% on Terminal-Bench 4.1 and 46.2% (at its default high setting) on FrontierCode 2.0 . For production-ready code, Neon handles multi-file debugging and refactoring across repositories with targeted, surgical edits. On SWE-sweep, it already leads on the mini-SWE harness (12.8%) and more than triples to a state-of-the-art 39.0% in Google Antigravity showing what the Antigravity harness unlocks.
